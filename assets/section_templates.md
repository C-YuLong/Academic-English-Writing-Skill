# Section Templates

These are reusable templates. Replace bracketed placeholders.

## Contribution Bullets

- `[Contribution 1]: This paper proposes [method/system/model], which [core novelty] for [problem].`
- `[Contribution 2]: This paper introduces [algorithm/architecture/flow] to [technical challenge], enabling [benefit].`
- `[Contribution 3]: This paper develops [implementation/prototype/tool] and evaluates it on [benchmarks/platforms].`
- `[Contribution 4]: Experimental results show that [method] achieves [quantified result] compared with [baseline].`

## Introduction Gap Paragraph

`Existing [category] methods have improved [metric/task] by [approach]. However, these methods typically assume [assumption] or require [resource/condition]. This assumption limits their effectiveness for [target scenario], where [constraint/challenge]. Therefore, a method that [desired property] is needed.`

## Related Work Category Paragraph

`[Category] methods address [problem] by [main idea]. For example, [Author] et al. [citation] propose [method], and [Author] et al. [citation] develop [method]. These methods are effective for [scenario]. However, they do not explicitly consider [gap relevant to this paper]. In contrast, the proposed [method] [key difference].`

## Method Overview Paragraph

`Figure [x] shows the overview of [method]. The input is [input], and the output is [output]. The method consists of three steps. First, [step 1]. Second, [step 2]. Third, [step 3]. These steps jointly address [challenge] by [mechanism].`

## Experiment Setup Paragraph

`The experiments evaluate [claim] using [benchmarks/datasets]. The proposed method is compared with [baselines]. The evaluation uses [metrics], where [metric direction and unit]. All experiments are conducted on [platform/tool/version] with [configuration].`

## Result Discussion Paragraph

`Table [x] reports [metric] for [methods] on [benchmarks]. The proposed method achieves [result] on average. The improvement is mainly due to [mechanism]. The largest improvement occurs on [case], where [reason]. These results support the claim that [contribution].`

## Reviewer Response Template

`Response: Thank you for the helpful comment. We agree that [issue] should be clarified. We have revised Section [x] to explain [specific content]. In particular, the revised manuscript now states that [clarification]. This change addresses the concern by [reason].`


## Venue/Field Calibration Note

`I calibrated the draft against recent [venue/field] papers from [year range]. The common pattern is [structure]. Recent papers typically frame the Introduction as [pattern], present contributions as [style], and validate claims through [experiment/evidence style]. I therefore structure the draft as [draft decision].`

## Paragraph Rewrite Template

`Topic sentence: [one sentence stating the topic and controlling idea]`

`Support logic: [evidence/example/result/citation/reasoning that supports the topic sentence]`

`Revised paragraph: [crisp academic paragraph with given-before-new flow]`

`Notes: [tense, article, modality, register, or flow changes]`

## Topic Sentence Patterns

- `Existing [method category] methods improve [metric], but they remain limited by [gap].`
- `[Proposed method] addresses [challenge] by [core mechanism].`
- `The experimental setup is designed to evaluate [claim] under [condition].`
- `The results show that [main result], primarily because [technical reason].`
- `[Prior work category] provides [benefit], whereas this paper focuses on [difference].`

## Crisp Sentence Repair Pattern

Weak:
`[Long sentence with unclear subject, several clauses, and buried main verb].`

Repair:
1. Identify the main subject.
2. Identify the main verb.
3. Move background information to a separate sentence or introductory phrase.
4. Put the main claim in a short declarative sentence.
5. Add a second sentence for qualification or evidence.
